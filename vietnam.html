Northern Vietnam

Hanoi

2–3 days recommended

A thousand-year-old capital of narrow Old Quarter lanes, lakeside cafés, and plastic stools set out for the best bowl of noodles you'll find on the trip.

Why it's special: Hanoi's Old Quarter still runs on its centuries-old guild-street layout — each lane historically sold one craft, and traces of that remain in what's sold there today.

Top Attractions

  • Old Quarter's 36 streets
  • Hoan Kiem Lake at sunrise
  • Train Street's narrow tracks
  • Temple of Literature

Recommended Activities

Street food walking tour Water puppet show Motorbike food crawl Egg coffee café hop

Famous Local Foods

Phở bò Bún chả Egg coffee Bánh mì

Culture & Traditions

Sidewalk life is the culture here — plastic-stool tea stalls and impromptu badminton games spill into the street each evening, so slow down and expect to share the sidewalk.

Best Time to VisitSeptember – November
Estimated Budget$35–$170 / day, by tier

Quang Ninh Province

Ha Long Bay

2 days (overnight cruise)

Nearly 2,000 limestone islands rising from emerald water — best experienced from the deck of an overnight junk boat, not a day-trip bus.

Why it's special: Waking up already anchored among the karsts, before the day-trip boats arrive, is a genuinely different experience from a rushed afternoon visit.

Top Attractions

  • Sung Sot (Surprise) Cave
  • Ti Top Island viewpoint
  • Luon Cave kayak route
  • Cat Ba Island's national park

Recommended Activities

Overnight junk boat cruise Sea kayaking Sunrise Tai Chi on deck Cooking demo aboard

Famous Local Foods

Fresh seafood hotpot Grilled squid Cha muc (squid cake) Ha Long spring rolls

Culture & Traditions

Floating fishing villages once dotted the bay for generations — most residents have since relocated ashore, but the traditions of net-fishing and pearl farming continue nearby.

Best Time to VisitMarch – May, Oct – Nov
Estimated Budget$80–$350 / day, by tier
Tour Duration2 days (1 night aboard)

Central Vietnam

Hoi An

2–3 days recommended

A preserved trading-port old town, lantern-lit along the river each night, with tailor shops that can turn around a custom suit before you fly out.

Why it's special: Hoi An's whole old town is a UNESCO site kept car-free after dark — the combination of lantern light, river reflections and no traffic is hard to replicate elsewhere.

Top Attractions

  • Japanese Covered Bridge
  • Old Town lantern streets
  • An Bang Beach
  • Central Market

Recommended Activities

Lantern boat ride Custom tailoring session Basket boat village tour Bicycle to rice paddies

Famous Local Foods

Cao lầu noodles White rose dumplings Bánh mì Phượng Mì Quảng

Culture & Traditions

On the 14th night of the lunar calendar, electric lights across the old town switch off in favor of lanterns alone — worth timing a visit around if dates allow.

Best Time to VisitFebruary – April
Estimated Budget$40–$200 / day, by tier
Tour Duration2–3 days

Southern Vietnam

Ho Chi Minh City

2–3 days recommended

Vietnam's fastest-moving city — scooters, sky bars and war-history sites within a few blocks of each other, plus the Mekong Delta an easy day trip away.

Why it's special: Few cities pack this much recent history and this much forward energy into one downtown — the contrast is part of what makes it worth a stop.

Top Attractions

  • War Remnants Museum
  • Cu Chi Tunnels
  • Ben Thanh Market
  • Notre-Dame Cathedral & Post Office

Recommended Activities

Mekong Delta day trip Rooftop bar hop Street food scooter tour Cu Chi Tunnels crawl

Famous Local Foods

Bánh xèo Cơm tấm Bún thịt nướng Vietnamese iced coffee

Culture & Traditions

Crossing the street here is a learned skill — walk at a steady pace and let the scooter stream flow around you rather than stopping or running.

Best Time to VisitDecember – April
