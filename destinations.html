Where We Go

Seven Countries, Endless Routes

Every destination below can stand alone or link into a longer Myanmar–Southeast Asia route — we'll plan the connections either way.

Myanmar

Home ground — and where every route begins

Our domestic base: thousands of Bagan temples at sunrise, Inle Lake's floating gardens, and Yangon's gold-lit Shwedagon Pagoda. Most journeys either start here or fold Myanmar into a longer Southeast Asia loop.

  • Bagan temple plain
  • Inle Lake villages
  • Shwedagon Pagoda
  • Mandalay's old capitals
Best season: November – March
Explore Myanmar

Thailand

Bangkok's energy, then the islands slow it down

Street-food alleys and river temples in Bangkok, then a straight flight to Phuket or Krabi for limestone cliffs and warm water. A reliable pairing with Myanmar for a first Southeast Asia trip.

  • Grand Palace, Bangkok
  • Phuket & Krabi coast
  • Chiang Mai temples
  • Floating markets
Best season: November – February
Explore Thailand

Vietnam

A coastline built for slow boat mornings

Hanoi's old quarter, an overnight cruise through Ha Long Bay's karst islands, and Hoi An's lantern-lit riverside in the evenings. Strong on food, better on scenery.

  • Ha Long Bay cruise
  • Hoi An old town
  • Hanoi street food
  • Ho Chi Minh City
Best season: February – April
Explore Vietnam

Singapore

A polished stopover with its own reasons to stay

Gardens by the Bay at night, hawker centers that rival any fine-dining table, and one of the region's smoothest airports — a natural two- or three-night add-on to any longer route.

  • Gardens by the Bay
  • Hawker food halls
  • Sentosa Island
  • Marina Bay Sands view
Best season: Year-round
Explore Singapore

Malaysia

Rainforest and skyline, closer together than you'd think

Kuala Lumpur's towers by night, the Cameron Highlands' tea terraces by day, and Penang's George Town for street art and Peranakan cooking in between.

  • Petronas Towers
  • Cameron Highlands
  • George Town, Penang
  • Batu Caves
Best season: December – February
Explore Malaysia

Indonesia

Bali still earns the hype — Ubud especially

Rice-terrace sunrises above Ubud, temple ceremonies on the coast, and private villas with a plunge pool for the honeymoon crowd. Our most-booked single-country trip.

  • Ubud rice terraces
  • Uluwatu sea temple
  • Seminyak beach clubs
  • Gili Islands
Best season: April – October
Explore Indonesia

Cambodia

Angkor at dawn, then the rest of the story

Angkor Wat before the crowds, jungle-reclaimed Ta Prohm, and Siem Reap's night markets after. Pairs naturally with Vietnam on a two-country loop.

  • Angkor Wat sunrise
  • Ta Prohm ruins
  • Siem Reap night markets
  • Tonlé Sap floating villages
Best season: November – February
Explore Cambodia
