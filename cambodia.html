Northwest Cambodia

Siem Reap & Angkor

3 days recommended

Gateway to Angkor Wat and its jungle-wrapped sister temples, with a lively Pub Street and night market to unwind in after a day of ruins.

Why it's special: Angkor's temple complex is the largest religious monument on earth — arriving before dawn, ahead of the tour buses, changes the whole experience.

Top Attractions

  • Angkor Wat sunrise
  • Ta Prohm's jungle-reclaimed ruins
  • Bayon Temple's stone faces
  • Pub Street & night market

Recommended Activities

Sunrise temple tour Bicycle temple circuit Apsara dance dinner show Night market food crawl

Famous Local Foods

Amok fish curry Khmer red curry Lok lak Num banh chok

Culture & Traditions

Angkor remains an active place of worship, not only a ruin — monks and local visitors still leave offerings inside several temples, so dress modestly and keep voices down near shrines.

Best Time to VisitNovember – February
Estimated Budget$40–$210 / day, by tier
Tour Duration3 days

Capital City

Phnom Penh

2 days recommended

A riverside capital of gilded royal architecture, French colonial streets, and a modern-history museum culture that meets its past directly.

Why it's special: The Royal Palace's gold-roofed complex sits a few blocks from candid, unflinching history museums — together they give the fullest picture of modern Cambodia.

Top Attractions

  • Royal Palace & Silver Pagoda
  • National Museum of Cambodia
  • Sisowath Quay riverside
  • Central Market (Phsar Thmei)

Recommended Activities

Royal Palace guided tour Riverside sunset cruise Central Market shopping Rooftop bar evening

Famous Local Foods

Khmer fish amok Bai sach chrouk Kuy teav noodle soup Palm sugar desserts

Culture & Traditions

The city's history sites are treated with particular solemnity by Cambodians — quiet, respectful behavior is expected, and photography guidelines are posted and worth following.

Best Time to VisitNovember – February
Estimated Budget$35–$180 / day, by tier
Tour Duration2 days

Central Cambodia

Tonlé Sap Lake

1 day recommended

Southeast Asia's largest freshwater lake, home to entire floating villages that rise and fall with the seasonal flood — a striking half-day trip from Siem Reap.

Why it's special: The lake's size changes dramatically between wet and dry seasons — homes, schools and even a floating basketball court adapt right along with it.

Top Attractions

  • Kampong Phluk floating village
  • Flooded mangrove forest
  • Floating school & market
  • Sunset over the lake

Recommended Activities

Floating village boat tour Mangrove kayaking Community visit Sunset lake cruise

Famous Local Foods

Fresh lake fish Prahok (fermented fish paste) Grilled river shrimp Sticky rice with fruit

Culture & Traditions

Many families here are of Vietnamese and Cham Muslim heritage and have fished the lake for generations — a reminder that Cambodia's culture extends well beyond the temples.

Best Time to VisitAugust – December (high water)
Estimated Budget$30–$150 / day, by tier
Tour Duration1 day

Southern Cambodia

Kampot

2 days recommended

A laid-back river town famous for its pepper plantations, colonial shophouses, and sunset boat rides past limestone karsts.

Why it's special: Kampot pepper is considered among the best in the world by chefs internationally, and the farms producing it are a short, easy visit from town.

Top Attractions

  • Kampot pepper farms
  • Old French quarter
  • Bokor Hill Station ruins
  • Kampot River sunset

Recommended Activities

Pepper farm tour Sunset river cruise Bokor Hill day trip Riverside cycling

Famous Local Foods

Kampot pepper crab Grilled river fish Durian (in season) Fresh fruit shakes

Culture & Traditions

The abandoned French hill station at Bokor sits eerily preserved above the clouds — a striking, low-key reminder of Cambodia's colonial-era history worth an hour of quiet wandering.

Best Time to VisitNovember – February
Estimated Budget$30–$150 / day, by tier
