FAQ

Common questions before you write in

How far in advance should I book?

For standard packages, 4–6 weeks is comfortable. Exclusive tier trips and peak-season dates (December–February) are best locked in 2–3 months out.

Do you handle visas for every destination?

Yes — visa assistance is included across all package tiers. We'll tell you exactly what's needed for your passport and itinerary.

Can I combine multiple countries in one trip?

It's most of what we do. Tell us your total days and we'll suggest a route that connects well instead of forcing in extra stops.

What if my flight is delayed or cancelled?

Our 24-hour support line rebooks transport and notifies your hotels and guides automatically — no need to manage it yourself.

Is travel insurance required?

It's optional but strongly recommended, and can be added to any package during booking.
