Central Thailand

Bangkok

3 days recommended

A river city running on canals, night markets and gold-spired temples, with some of the best street food on the planet a few steps from any hotel.

Why it's special: Bangkok compresses ancient and modern Thailand into one skyline — royal temples a short longtail-boat ride from rooftop bars and mall-sized night markets.

Top Attractions

  • Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew
  • Wat Arun at sunset, river-side
  • Chatuchak Weekend Market
  • Chinatown's Yaowarat Road

Recommended Activities

Chao Phraya river cruise Street food walking tour Thai cooking class Rooftop bar evening

Famous Local Foods

Pad Thai Tom yum goong Mango sticky rice Boat noodles

Culture & Traditions

Temples require shoulders and knees covered, and monks are not to be touched by women — sarongs are available on loan at most major sites for anyone caught underdressed.

Best Time to VisitNovember – February
Estimated Budget$45–$210 / day, by tier

Andaman Coast

Phuket

3–4 days recommended

Thailand's largest island — limestone cliffs, warm turquoise water, and a range that runs from backpacker beach bars to five-star cliffside resorts.

Why it's special: Phuket works as a base for island-hopping to Phi Phi and the Phang Nga Bay karsts, so one stay covers several very different beach days.

Top Attractions

  • Phi Phi Islands day trip
  • Phang Nga Bay limestone karsts
  • Old Phuket Town's Sino-Portuguese streets
  • Big Buddha viewpoint

Recommended Activities

Island-hopping speedboat Snorkeling & diving Sea kayaking sea caves Sunset catamaran cruise

Famous Local Foods

Phuket lobster Moo hong (braised pork) Southern-style curry O-aew dessert

Culture & Traditions

Old Phuket Town's architecture reflects generations of Chinese and Portuguese traders — the annual Vegetarian Festival still draws crowds for its street processions each October.

Best Time to VisitNovember – April
Estimated Budget$60–$320 / day, by tier
Tour Duration3–4 days

Northern Thailand

Chiang Mai

3 days recommended

A moated old city of temple bells and jungle-covered mountains, slower and cooler than Bangkok, with a night market that fills the streets every evening.

Why it's special: Chiang Mai puts hill-tribe villages, ethical elephant sanctuaries and 700-year-old temples within an hour of a walkable, low-rise city center.

Top Attractions

  • Wat Phra That Doi Suthep
  • Old City temple circuit
  • Sunday Walking Street market
  • Mae Sa elephant sanctuary

Recommended Activities

Ethical elephant visit Thai cooking class Doi Inthanon day trip Muay Thai lesson

Famous Local Foods

Khao soi Sai oua (herb sausage) Nam prik noom Khanom jeen

Culture & Traditions

Chiang Mai's Yi Peng lantern festival (November) fills the sky with floating paper lanterns — book accommodation months ahead if your dates can align with it.

Best Time to VisitNovember – February
Estimated Budget$40–$190 / day, by tier
Tour Duration3 days

Andaman Coast

Krabi

3 days recommended

Sheer limestone cliffs rising straight out of the sea, with rock climbers and boat-only beaches taking the place of Phuket's resort strip.

Why it's special: Railay Beach has no road access at all — every arrival is by longtail boat, which keeps the pace slower than almost anywhere else on this coast.

Top Attractions

  • Railay Beach & viewpoint
  • Emerald Pool & Hot Springs
  • Tiger Cave Temple stairway
  • Four Islands boat tour

Recommended Activities

Rock climbing Longtail island tour Sea cave kayaking Sunset viewpoint hike

Famous Local Foods

Grilled river prawns Southern Thai curry Fresh mango salad Coconut ice cream

Culture & Traditions

Fishing villages along the mangrove coast still run on tides more than clocks — boat schedules shift with the water, so mornings start early during low tide season.

Best Time to VisitDecember – March
Estimated Budget$50–$260 / day, by tier
