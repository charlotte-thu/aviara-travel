About Aviara

Built by People Who'd Rather Be Traveling

Aviara started as three planners' shared notebook of good routes through Myanmar. It's now a full team — still working from the same notebook.

Our Story

From one Yangon office to routes across seven countries

Aviara opened as a small Yangon desk booking domestic trips for a handful of regulars. As those travelers asked for Thailand, then Vietnam, then Bali, we built the same care into international routes — always planned by people who'd taken the trip themselves first.

Today that same principle holds: nothing goes into an itinerary that our own team hasn't checked in person.

Our Mission

To remove the friction from regional travel — one planner, one itinerary, every detail handled before you land.

Our Vision

To be the first call for anyone planning a Myanmar or Southeast Asia trip, from a weekend beach stop to a month-long loop.

Why Aviara

Local planners, transparent pricing, and a support line that answers at 2am if your connecting flight doesn't.
