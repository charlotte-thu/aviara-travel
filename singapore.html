Downtown Core

Marina Bay

1–2 days recommended

Singapore's postcard skyline — the three-tower Marina Bay Sands, a nightly light show over the water, and rooftop bars with views to match.

Why it's special: Everything is walkable along the waterfront promenade, so a single evening covers the skyline view, dinner and a light show without needing transport.

Top Attractions

  • Marina Bay Sands SkyPark
  • Merlion Park
  • Spectra light & water show
  • Helix Bridge night walk

Recommended Activities

SkyPark observation deck Rooftop bar evening Bumboat river ride Waterfront promenade walk

Famous Local Foods

Chili crab Laksa Satay by the bay Kaya toast

Culture & Traditions

Marina Bay sits on reclaimed land completed within living memory — a good reminder of how deliberately and quickly Singapore's skyline was built, and still is.

Resort Island

Sentosa Island

1–2 days recommended

A resort island a short cable-car ride from downtown — theme parks, man-made beaches and aquariums packed into a few square kilometers.

Why it's special: Sentosa condenses a full family theme-park day, a beach afternoon and a cable-car ride into one short island hop from the CBD.

Top Attractions

  • Universal Studios Singapore
  • S.E.A. Aquarium
  • Siloso Beach
  • Sentosa cable car ride

Recommended Activities

Theme park day pass Beach club afternoon Cable car ride Evening beach bonfire

Famous Local Foods

Beach club seafood Ice kacang BBQ skewers Fresh coconut

Culture & Traditions

Sentosa's name means "peace and tranquility" in Malay — chosen deliberately when the island was redeveloped from a former colonial-era military site into a resort destination.

Outram

Chinatown

1 day recommended

Painted shophouses, incense-filled temples and a hawker center that holds a Michelin star — a compact, walkable slice of old Singapore.

Why it's special: Three faiths share this small district peacefully — a Hindu temple, a mosque and a Buddhist relic temple all sit within a few minutes' walk of each other.

Top Attractions

  • Buddha Tooth Relic Temple
  • Sri Mariamman Temple
  • Maxwell Food Centre
  • Ann Siang Hill shophouses

Recommended Activities

Hawker center food tour Temple heritage walk Traditional medicine shop visit Night market browsing

Famous Local Foods

Hainanese chicken rice Char kway teow Bak kut teh Tau huay (soy pudding)

Culture & Traditions

Lunar New Year transforms Chinatown's streets into a lantern-lit market weeks in advance — one of the best times to visit if dates line up, though also the busiest.

Marina South

Gardens by the Bay

1 day recommended

A 250-acre nature park of climate-controlled domes and towering Supertrees that light up after dark for a nightly sound-and-light show.

Why it's special: The Cloud Forest dome recreates a misty mountain rainforest inside a glass structure — a genuinely surprising contrast to the tropical heat outside.

Top Attractions

  • Supertree Grove
  • Cloud Forest dome
  • Flower Dome
  • OCBC Skyway walkway

Recommended Activities

Garden Rhapsody light show Skyway treetop walk Dome exhibits Picnic on the Meadow

Famous Local Foods

Garden café bites Local dessert stalls Fresh fruit juice Satay Street nearby

Culture & Traditions

The park is part of Singapore's "City in a Garden" vision — a deliberate policy choice to weave dense greenery into a high-density city rather than push it to the outskirts.
